## Introduction

Sakteng Wildlife Sanctuary occupies a unique place in [Bhutan]({{ 'what-haunts-bhutans-high-valleys/' | relative_url }})’s monster folklore because it is the location most closely associated with the Migoi, the country’s famous wild-man or yeti tradition. For many visitors, it is the place that inspired headlines claiming Bhutan had created a protected area for a legendary creature. The reality is more interesting than the myth. Sakteng is a genuine conservation landscape in eastern Bhutan, home to rare wildlife, mountain forests, alpine meadows and the semi-nomadic Brokpa people. At the same time, local Migoi traditions are so deeply woven into the cultural identity of the region that the sanctuary became internationally known as “Yeti country”.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.visitbhutan.com/destination-detail.php?id=13" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitbhutan.com">[visitbhutan.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitbhutan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Merak &amp; SaktengThe Sakteng Wildlife Sanctuary was primarily set up to protect the elusive Migoi, or yeti, which is historically rooted in...</span></span></span>

## Why Merak and Sakteng Became the Story's Centre

The Migoi is associated with many parts of the Himalayas, but in Bhutan the strongest geographical link is the highland region around Merak and Sakteng in Trashigang District. These settlements lie within a remote landscape of forests, grazing grounds and mountain passes where the Brokpa people have traditionally herded livestock and moved seasonally across difficult terrain.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/5701/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[unesco.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">It encompasses a total area of 740.60 square kilometer.Read more...</span></span></span>

Several factors helped make the area the centre of Bhutan's Migoi tradition:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* The region remained isolated from the rest of Bhutan for much of its history.
* Herders routinely travelled through forests and high-altitude pastures where unusual tracks, sounds and fleeting sightings could be interpreted through local folklore.
* Brokpa traditions preserved stories of powerful wild beings that occupied remote parts of the landscape.
* The terrain itself feels mysterious, with dense forests, frequent mist and large areas that were historically difficult to access.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.visitbhutan.com/destination-detail.php?id=13" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitbhutan.com">[visitbhutan.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitbhutan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Merak &amp; SaktengThe Sakteng Wildlife Sanctuary was primarily set up to protect the elusive Migoi, or yeti, which is historically rooted in...</span></span></span>

</div>

Unlike many modern cryptid stories that began with newspaper reports or internet rumours, the Migoi tradition in Merak and Sakteng emerged from local oral culture. The creature functions not only as a possible mystery animal but also as part of a broader understanding of wilderness and the relationship between people and remote mountain environments.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.visitbhutan.com/destination-detail.php?id=13" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitbhutan.com">[visitbhutan.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitbhutan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Merak &amp; SaktengThe Sakteng Wildlife Sanctuary was primarily set up to protect the elusive Migoi, or yeti, which is historically rooted in...</span></span></span>

## Did Bhutan Really Create a Sanctuary for a Yeti?

This is the question that attracts most international attention.

Many tourism publications, travel writers and media reports have repeated the claim that Sakteng Wildlife Sanctuary was established specifically to protect Migoi habitat. Bhutan’s official tourism material has also highlighted that the sanctuary was set up in part because of the creature’s importance within Brokpa belief and ritual traditions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.visitbhutan.com/destination-detail.php?id=13" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitbhutan.com">[visitbhutan.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitbhutan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Merak &amp; SaktengThe Sakteng Wildlife Sanctuary was primarily set up to protect the elusive Migoi, or yeti, which is historically rooted in...</span></span></span>

However, the phrase can be misleading if taken literally.

The sanctuary was established as part of Bhutan’s protected-area network and serves conventional conservation goals: preserving ecosystems, protecting biodiversity and supporting traditional highland culture. Official descriptions emphasise its forests, river systems, alpine meadows and remarkable biological diversity. UNESCO’s tentative World Heritage description focuses on landscape connectivity, ecological value and cultural heritage rather than on proving the existence of a cryptid.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/5701/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">It encompasses a total area of 740.60 square kilometer.Read more...</span></span></span>

A more accurate interpretation is that Bhutan chose not to separate cultural beliefs from environmental protection. Local Migoi traditions formed part of the area's identity, so the sanctuary's creation acknowledged those traditions while protecting the landscape in which they arose. The protected habitat is unquestionably real even if the legendary resident remains unverified.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.visitbhutan.com/destination-detail.php?id=13" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitbhutan.com">[visitbhutan.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitbhutan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Merak &amp; SaktengThe Sakteng Wildlife Sanctuary was primarily set up to protect the elusive Migoi, or yeti, which is historically rooted in...</span></span></span>

## What the Sanctuary Officially Protects

If visitors arrive expecting a park devoted entirely to yeti hunting, they quickly discover that Sakteng's actual conservation mission is far broader.

The sanctuary covers roughly 740 square kilometres in eastern Bhutan and contains ecosystems ranging from warm broadleaf forests to alpine meadows at elevations exceeding 4,000 metres. It forms part of Bhutan’s larger network of biological corridors designed to connect wildlife populations across the country.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/5701/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whc.unesco.org">[UNESCO World Heritage Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whc.unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">It encompasses a total area of 740.60 square kilometer.Read more...</span></span></span>

Its ecological importance includes:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Habitat for the endangered red panda.
* Populations of Himalayan black bear, wild dog, leopard and other mountain mammals.
* Rich bird diversity, including species with limited regional distributions.
* Extensive rhododendron habitats and eastern Himalayan plant communities.
* Protection of one of Bhutan’s most distinctive highland cultural landscapes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.visitbhutan.com/destination-detail.php?id=13" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitbhutan.com">[visitbhutan.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitbhutan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Merak &amp; SaktengThe Sakteng Wildlife Sanctuary was primarily set up to protect the elusive Migoi, or yeti, which is historically rooted in...</span></span></span>

</div>

Equally important is the Brokpa cultural heritage. The sanctuary is unusual because conservation authorities must manage not only wildlife and forests but also the needs of communities whose livelihoods depend on pastoral traditions. In practice, preserving the landscape means preserving a living culture as well.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.bhutanbirding.com/protected-areas/sakteng-wildlife-sanctuary/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bhutanbirding.com">[bhutanbirding.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bhutanbirding.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sakteng Wildlife Sanctuary &#124; Wild Nature QuestSakteng Wildlife Sanctuary, situated in the easternmost part of Bhutan, is a hidden gem kno...</span></span></span>

## How Tourism Turns Habitat Into Legend

Most travellers encounter the Migoi through trekking routes linking Merak and Sakteng rather than through eyewitness reports.

Tourism promotion often presents the sanctuary as the homeland of Bhutan's yeti. Trekking itineraries, visitor guides and travel features frequently blend wildlife viewing, Brokpa culture and [Migoi folklore]({{ 'migoi-folklore/' | relative_url }}) into a single experience. The result is a landscape marketed not just as beautiful but as mysterious.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://abovethehimalaya.com/merak-sakteng-trek" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abovethehimalaya.com">[abovethehimalaya.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abovethehimalaya.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">TravelMerak Sakteng Trek Bhutan: Route, Itinerary &amp; Travel GuidePlan your Merak Sakteng Trek with our complete Bhutan travel guide - incl...</span></span></span>

## Why the Migoi Remains Unresolved

Despite its fame, Sakteng has not produced convincing scientific [evidence]({{ 'evidence/' | relative_url }}) for an unknown ape-like species.

No accepted body, skeleton, clear genetic sample or verified photograph has emerged from the sanctuary. The strongest support for the Migoi comes from local testimony, traditional stories and occasional claims of sightings or tracks. [himalaya2000.com](https://www.himalaya2000.com/bhutan/wildlife-sanctuaries/sakteng.html)

Sceptical explanations generally point toward a combination of factors:

* Misidentification of known wildlife such as bears.
* Distorted tracks in snow or mud.
* Brief observations in poor visibility.
* Storytelling traditions passed through generations.
* The tendency of remote landscapes to encourage mystery narratives. [himalaya2000.com](https://www.himalaya2000.com/bhutan/wildlife-sanctuaries/sakteng.html)

Yet the absence of evidence has not diminished the sanctuary's reputation. In many ways, uncertainty is what keeps the story alive. Sakteng remains compelling because it is simultaneously a real protected ecosystem, a homeland of distinctive Brokpa culture and the landscape most closely associated with Bhutan's enduring Migoi tradition.
