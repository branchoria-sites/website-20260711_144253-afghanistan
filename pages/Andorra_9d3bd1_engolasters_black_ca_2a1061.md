## Introduction

Lake Engolasters is often described as [Andorra]({{ 'where-are-andorras-monsters-hiding/' | relative_url }})’s closest equivalent to a classic monster-haunted lake, yet its most famous legend is not about a hidden creature rising from the water. Instead, the lake is remembered for a striking tale of witches, a drowned settlement and men transformed into black cats. In Andorran folklore, the mystery of Engolasters lies less in an unknown animal and more in a warning about curiosity, temptation and respect for forbidden places. The story has survived long after the supposed witches vanished, becoming one of the country’s best-known supernatural traditions and a rare example of a lake legend where black cats, rather than monsters, take centre stage.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitandorra.com/en/nature--sports/hiking-route-llac-d-engolasters/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitandorra.com">[Visit Andorra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitandorra.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visit AndorraHiking route: Llac d&#x27;EngolastersTo punish them, the witches turned all “voyeurs” into black cats. ## The Drowned Village and Spying Men

Several versions of the Engolasters legend begin before the lake became a place of witchcraft. According to local tradition, a settlement once stood where the water now lies. One popular account tells of a traveller or pilgrim who was refused charity by the inhabitants. Only a young woman showed kindness. After warning her to flee, the stranger unleashed a divine punishment that drowned the village and created the lake. Variations of the story differ in detail, but all explain the lake as the result of a moral failing followed by supernatural retribution.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Engolasters" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[wikipedia.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lake Engolasters</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lake Engolasters</span></span></span>

Once the lake existed, it became associated with nocturnal gatherings of witches. Folklore describes women meeting at night beside the water, bathing naked and conducting magical ceremonies away from the villages below. These gatherings attracted attention from local men who attempted to sneak up to the lake and watch. The men knew the risk: anyone caught spying could expect a magical punishment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitandorra.com/en/nature--sports/hiking-route-llac-d-engolasters/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitandorra.com">[Visit Andorra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitandorra.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visit AndorraHiking route: Llac d&#x27;EngolastersTo punish them, the witches turned all “voyeurs” into black cats. The tale follows a familiar folkloric pattern found across Europe. Secret supernatural meetings are hidden from ordinary people, and those who violate the boundary between the human world and the enchanted world suffer consequences. At Engolasters, however, the punishment took an unusually feline form.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://perennialpyrenees.com/2017/04/24/weekly-article-1-the-witches-of-engolasters-lake/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: perennialpyrenees.com">[Perennial Pyrenees]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">perennialpyrenees.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">weekly article 1 the witches of engolasters lake</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">turn them into black cats. ## Black Cats as Curse, Not Phantom Panthers

Unlike stories of phantom big cats reported elsewhere in Europe, the black cats of Engolasters were never presented as mysterious animals roaming the mountains. They were transformed humans.

According to the most commonly repeated version of the legend, witches caught the voyeurs and turned them into black cats. Tourism materials produced in Andorra still recount this detail, describing how the men who crept to the lakeshore to watch the witches were transformed as punishment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitandorra.com/en/nature--sports/hiking-route-llac-d-engolasters/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitandorra.com">[Visit Andorra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitandorra.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visit AndorraHiking route: Llac d&#x27;EngolastersTo punish them, the witches turned all “voyeurs” into black cats. Some folkloric retellings add extra details. Young men supposedly protected themselves with laurel leaves before climbing towards the lake, believing this would shield them from witchcraft. It failed. Guard witches intercepted them, hurled enchanted blades and transformed them into black cats. Drawn towards the sabbath-like celebrations, the cats then clung to the witches and unwillingly became part of the night's ceremonies. By morning they returned to human form, exhausted and unable to remember what had happened.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://perennialpyrenees.com/2017/04/24/weekly-article-1-the-witches-of-engolasters-lake/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: perennialpyrenees.com">[Perennial Pyrenees]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">perennialpyrenees.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">weekly article 1 the witches of engolasters lake</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">turn them into black cats. For readers interested in cryptid traditions, this distinction is important. The black cats of Engolasters are not Andorra’s version of the British phantom panther or a mystery predator hidden in the wild. They belong to a much older European tradition linking black cats and witchcraft. Across medieval and early modern Europe, black cats were commonly associated with witches, magical transformation and supernatural companions. The Engolasters story adapts that wider folklore into a specifically Andorran landscape.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://phs-spca.org/black-cat-myths/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: phs-spca.org">[Peninsula Humane Society &amp; SPCA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">phs-spca.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Peninsula Humane Society &amp; SPCABlack Cat Myths</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">March 9, 2018 — During the Middle Ages, much of Europe believed that witches would shapeshift into black cats, and that witches&#x27; black ca...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 9, 2018</span></span></span>

As a result, the legend sits on the edge of cryptid culture without really becoming a cryptid story. The cats are memorable because they are cursed people, not because anyone believed they represented an undiscovered animal species.

## Why the Legend Attached to Engolasters

The setting helps explain the story’s endurance. Lake Engolasters occupies a dramatic position above the valley in the parish of Encamp. Surrounded by forests and mountain slopes, it is exactly the sort of isolated location where stories of hidden gatherings and supernatural encounters flourish.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Engolasters" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lake Engolasters</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lake Engolasters</span></span></span>

The wider Pyrenees also possess a long history of witch traditions. Folklore sites, tales of magical practitioners and stories of demonic gatherings appear throughout the mountain region. Engolasters therefore fits into a broader cultural landscape rather than standing entirely alone. The lake became one local stage upon which larger Pyrenean beliefs about witches, sabbaths and magical punishment were projected.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://perennialpyrenees.com/2017/04/24/weekly-article-1-the-witches-of-engolasters-lake/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: perennialpyrenees.com">[Perennial Pyrenees]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">perennialpyrenees.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">weekly article 1 the witches of engolasters lake</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">turn them into black cats. The cats would in turn be strangely attracted by the smell of the dancing witches and cling to their backs...</span></span></span>

What makes the Engolasters version memorable is the combination of elements. Many European witch legends involve curses or shape-changing, but fewer feature a recurring transformation of curious onlookers into black cats. That detail gives the story a distinctive identity within Andorran folklore and explains why it remains one of the country's most frequently retold supernatural tales.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://perennialpyrenees.com/2017/04/24/weekly-article-1-the-witches-of-engolasters-lake/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: perennialpyrenees.com">[Perennial Pyrenees]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">perennialpyrenees.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">weekly article 1 the witches of engolasters lake</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">turn them into black cats. ## Hydroelectric Modernisation and the Enchanted Lake

An unusual feature of the Engolasters legend is that it acquired a clear ending.

Modern retellings often state that the witches disappeared at the beginning of the twentieth century. The timing is linked to major changes around the lake, particularly the construction of hydroelectric infrastructure and later technological developments in the area. Tourist and heritage sources repeatedly note that the witches were said to abandon the lake when the dam, hydroelectric works and associated modern projects transformed the landscape.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitandorra.com/en/nature--sports/hiking-route-llac-d-engolasters/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitandorra.com">[visitandorra.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitandorra.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Visit AndorraHiking route: Llac d&#x27;EngolastersTo punish them, the witches turned all “voyeurs” into black cats. Luckily, the witches aband...</span></span></span>

Historically, Lake Engolasters became a key part of Andorra’s early hydroelectric development. The dam and associated facilities altered the site physically and symbolically. A place once imagined as remote and enchanted became tied to industry, electricity generation and modern communications.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://visitandorra.com/content/dam/visitandorra/nl/assets/documents/cfs/itinerary/hiking-route-llac-d-engolasters/13-engolasters-lake-eng.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitandorra.com">[Visit Andorra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitandorra.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">aught they would be turned into a black cat...</span></span></span>

This transition is one reason the legend remains appealing today. Rather than ending with the destruction of folklore, the story presents modernisation itself as the force that finally drove away the witches. The supernatural retreats, the infrastructure arrives, and the lake survives as both a practical resource and a place of imagination.

For modern visitors, Lake Engolasters is primarily a hiking destination, a scenic reservoir and a well-known stop near Encamp.
