## Introduction

Melusina is not a cryptid in the modern sense of an unknown animal reported by witnesses. Instead, she is [Luxembourg]({{ 'what-monsters-haunt-luxembourgs-small/' | relative_url }})'s most famous legendary creature: a water spirit whose story is woven into the founding myth of Luxembourg City itself. For many Luxembourgers, the legend explains why the city emerged on the rocky Bock promontory above the River Alzette and why the landscape still feels haunted by memory, secrecy and enchantment. The tale combines romance, betrayal and transformation, turning a medieval foundation story into a living piece of local identity. Today, Melusina remains visible in statues, guided tours, museums and folklore, making her one of the most enduring creature figures associated with Luxembourg.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.luxembourg-city.com/en/about-luxembourg-city/meng-stad-my-city/details/melusina-and-siegfried" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: luxembourg-city.com">[Luxembourg City]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">luxembourg-city.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Luxembourg CityMelusina and Siegfried: A Luxembourgish Love StoryCount Siegfried is considered the founder of Luxembourg. ## The Siegfried Marriage Legend

The best-known version of the story begins with Count Siegfried, the historical nobleman whose acquisition of the Bock promontory in 963 is traditionally regarded as the starting point of Luxembourg City. According to legend, while travelling through the Alzette valley he encountered a mysterious and extraordinarily beautiful woman named Melusina. They fell in love, but she agreed to marry him only under strict conditions. Most versions say that she demanded complete privacy every Saturday and forbade her husband from seeing her on that day.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.luxembourg-city.com/en/about-luxembourg-city/meng-stad-my-city/details/melusina-and-siegfried" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: luxembourg-city.com">[luxembourg-city.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">luxembourg-city.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Luxembourg CityMelusina and Siegfried: A Luxembourgish Love StoryCount Siegfried is considered the founder of Luxembourg. For years Siegfried honoured the promise. Eventually, however, curiosity overcame him. He secretly watched Melusina during one of her private Saturdays and discovered her hidden nature. In Luxembourg's most familiar retelling, her lower body became that of a fish or mermaid. Having been betrayed, she vanished forever into the waters of the Alzette below the Bock.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.luxembourg-city.com/en/place/monument/melusina-statue" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: luxembourg-city.com">[luxembourg-city.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">luxembourg-city.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Luxembourg CityMelusina statueThe legend of Melusina. The legend follows a pattern found in many European supernatural marriage tales. A human enters a relationship with a being from another realm, receives a prohibition, breaks it, and loses the supernatural spouse. What makes the Luxembourg version distinctive is that the story is attached directly to a real city, a real founder and a recognisable landscape.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melusine" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">March 4, 2026 — Melusine or Melusina is a figure of European folklore, a female spirit of fresh water in a holy well or river.Read more...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 4, 2026</span></span></span>

## Why a Water Spirit Became the City's Creature

Melusina belongs to a wider European family of freshwater spirit traditions. Across medieval folklore, similar figures appear as women connected to rivers, springs and hidden waters. They are often beautiful, powerful and partly human, yet remain linked to forces beyond ordinary society.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melusine" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">March 4, 2026 — Melusine or Melusina is a figure of European folklore, a female spirit of fresh water in a holy well or river.Read more...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 4, 2026</span></span></span>

In Luxembourg, the water-spirit element carries several symbolic meanings.

**She represents the hidden power of place.** The Alzette valley and the cliffs of the Bock created the natural setting from which Luxembourg developed. By placing a supernatural being within that landscape, the legend turns geography into something alive and meaningful.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.luxembourg-city.com/en/about-luxembourg-city/meng-stad-my-city/details/melusina-and-siegfried" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: luxembourg-city.com">[luxembourg-city.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">luxembourg-city.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Luxembourg CityMelusina and Siegfried: A Luxembourgish Love StoryCount Siegfried is considered the founder of Luxembourg. **She embodies mystery and boundaries.** The central rule of the story is not to cross a forbidden line. Siegfried's failure is not violence or warfare but curiosity. The tragedy occurs because the hidden world refuses to be fully exposed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://chronicle.lu/category/at-home/51100-luxembourg-myths-siegfried-melusina" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: chronicle.lu">[Chronicle.lu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">chronicle.lu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">51100 luxembourg myths siegfried melusina</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Luxembourg Myths: Siegfried &amp; Melusina20 Aug 2024 — The best-known Luxembourgish myth is that of Melusina, the mermaid who married Count...</span></span></span>

**She connects prosperity with sacrifice.** In many tellings, Melusina's presence is tied to the success of Luxembourg's foundation. The city gains its future, but the supernatural bride is ultimately lost. The legend suggests that great achievements often come with hidden costs.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://expopavilion.lu/2021/06/23/melusina-and-the-founding-myth-of-luxembourg-city/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: expopavilion.lu">[Luxembourg @ Expo 2025 Osaka]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">expopavilion.lu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">melusina and the founding myth of luxembourg city</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Luxembourg @ Expo 2025 OsakaMelusina and the founding myth of Luxembourg City23 Jun 2021 — Melusina and the founding myth of Luxembourg C...</span></span></span>

Unlike a monster that threatens a community, Melusina is both benefactor and outsider. She helps create the conditions for the city's existence while never fully belonging to the human world.

## The Alzette, the Bock and City Identity

The power of the legend comes from its attachment to specific locations that still exist today. The Alzette River curves beneath the old city, while the Bock promontory rises dramatically above it. Historically, this rocky position offered exceptional defensive advantages and became the site associated with Luxembourg's earliest castle and later fortifications.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.luxembourg-city.com/en/about-luxembourg-city/meng-stad-my-city/details/melusina-and-siegfried" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: luxembourg-city.com">[luxembourg-city.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">luxembourg-city.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Luxembourg CityMelusina and Siegfried: A Luxembourgish Love StoryCount Siegfried is considered the founder of Luxembourg. The legend maps directly onto these landmarks. Guided tours retrace Melusina's story through the Alzette valley, the Bock and the old fortifications, encouraging visitors to view the landscape through the lens of folklore rather than military history alone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.luxembourg-city.com/en/place/tour/Siegfried-melusina" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: luxembourg-city.com">[Luxembourg City]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">luxembourg-city.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Luxembourg CitySiegfried &amp; MelusinaThis guided tour of approximately one hour invites you to immerse yourself in one of Luxembourg&#x27;s most...</span></span></span>

This geographical grounding helps explain why Melusina survived while many medieval legends faded. Visitors can stand beside the river where she supposedly disappeared. They can look up at the cliffs associated with Siegfried's castle. The story remains visible because the setting remains visible.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.luxembourg-city.com/en/about-luxembourg-city/meng-stad-my-city/details/melusina-and-siegfried" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: luxembourg-city.com">[Luxembourg City]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">luxembourg-city.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Luxembourg CityMelusina and Siegfried: A Luxembourgish Love StoryCount Siegfried is considered the founder of Luxembourg. In effect, Melusina transforms ordinary features of the city into legendary landmarks. The river becomes an entrance to another world, and the rock becomes more than a defensive position: it becomes the stage on which Luxembourg's founding myth unfolds.

## From Hidden Water Spirit to Public Landmark

Over time, Melusina evolved from a folkloric figure into a public symbol of Luxembourg itself. One of the clearest examples is the modern Melusina statue installed beside the Alzette in the Grund district in 2015. The artwork places the legendary mermaid directly back into the landscape where her story is set.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.luxembourg-city.com/en/place/monument/melusina-statue" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: luxembourg-city.com">[Luxembourg City]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">luxembourg-city.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Luxembourg CityMelusina statueThe legend of Melusina. The legend also appears in educational programmes, museum activities, tourism campaigns and cultural events. Rather than being treated as a literal being whose existence must be proven, Melusina functions as a shared cultural character through whom Luxembourg tells its own story.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://citymuseum.lu/en/event/melusine-and-siegfried/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: citymuseum.lu">[citymuseum.lu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">citymuseum.lu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lëtzebuerg City MuseumMelusine and SiegfriedThe aim is to introduce pupils to a major legend of the Luxembourgish heritage. This shift is important from a folklore perspective. Many legendary creatures remain confined to old texts or oral traditions. Melusina instead became part of the public image of the capital city.
