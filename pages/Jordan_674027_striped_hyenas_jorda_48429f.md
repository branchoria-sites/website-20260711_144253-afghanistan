## Introduction

Many of [Jordan]({{ 'what-haunts-jordans-desert-edges/' | relative_url }})’s mystery-animal stories begin with a perfectly real creature seen under imperfect conditions. Few animals illustrate this better than the striped hyena. Found across Jordan and active mainly at night, the striped hyena is elusive, silent for long periods, unexpectedly large when encountered at close range, and associated with carcasses and lonely places. Those traits have helped turn an ordinary wild mammal into a source of fear, rumour and monster-like storytelling. Rather than being a hidden cryptid, the striped hyena shows how wildlife, folklore and misidentification can merge into something that feels supernatural. Studies of Jordanian hyenas repeatedly note that local myths and superstitions surround the species, even though its ecology reveals an animal that is primarily a scavenger rather than a hunter of people.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/237574526_Status_and_ecology_of_the_Striped_Hyaena_Hyaena_hyaena_in_Jordan" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchgate.net">[ResearchGate]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchgate.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ResearchGate(PDF) Status and ecology of the Striped Hyaena...... ## Why Do Hyenas Feel So Uncanny?

The striped hyena occupies an unusual place in the imagination. Wolves, foxes and wild cats fit familiar categories, but hyenas seem harder to place. Their sloping backs, shaggy coats, powerful jaws and strange gait can appear unsettling, especially in poor light.

Across the Middle East, hyenas have long attracted folklore that portrays them as mysterious, deceptive or connected to supernatural forces. Historical traditions from the wider region often associated striped hyenas with jinn, graveyards and unusual magical powers. Some beliefs even claimed that hyenas could mesmerise people or influence human behaviour.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Striped_hyena" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Striped hyena</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">May 11, 2026 — In folklore, religion, and mythology · Predation on livestock and crops · Attacks on humans and grave desecration · Huntin...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 11, 2026</span></span></span>

In Jordan, researchers studying the species found that myths remain remarkably persistent. Reported beliefs included claims that hyenas can cause hallucinations or possess unusual transformative abilities. These stories are not evidence of supernatural behaviour, but they help explain why sightings of a real animal can quickly acquire a legendary quality.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/237574526_Status_and_ecology_of_the_Striped_Hyaena_Hyaena_hyaena_in_Jordan" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchgate.net">[ResearchGate]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchgate.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ResearchGate(PDF) Status and ecology of the Striped Hyaena...... ## The Real Striped Hyena in Jordan

The reality is both less frightening and more interesting than the folklore. The striped hyena is one of Jordan’s native carnivores and occurs across much of the country rather than being confined to a single remote region. ## Sounds, Silhouettes and Night Sightings

Most people do not encounter a striped hyena in bright daylight. They glimpse one briefly at dusk, hear movement near a road, or notice a strange shape crossing a track after dark.

This is exactly the sort of situation that produces mystery-beast reports.

A striped hyena can look much larger than it really is. When alarmed, it raises the long mane running along its neck and back, increasing its apparent size dramatically. In darkness, a raised mane and broad shoulders can create a silhouette that seems unlike any familiar animal.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tsavotrust.org/what-are-the-different-types-of-hyena/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tsavotrust.org">[Tsavo Trust]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tsavotrust.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Tsavo TrustWhat are the different types of hyena?The four living Hyena species · 1. Its behaviour adds to the effect. Unlike wolves, which often move with a recognisable canine profile, a hyena’s uneven body proportions create a distinctive, almost unnatural outline. Brief sightings can therefore leave witnesses uncertain about what they have seen.

The setting matters too. Jordan’s deserts, wadis, rocky escarpments and isolated ruins are environments where visibility is limited and distance is difficult to judge. A nocturnal scavenger appearing suddenly in such places can feel far more mysterious than the same animal viewed in daylight. That uncertainty is often the starting point for local monster stories.

## Fear, Misidentification and Modern Understanding

Modern conservationists often describe the striped hyena as one of the world's most misunderstood carnivores. The species is generally shy around people, and direct attacks are exceptionally rare compared with its reputation. Yet cultural fears continue to shape how communities view it. Research from several regions has found that superstition and negative folklore can influence attitudes towards hyenas and sometimes contribute to persecution of the animals.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ifaw.org/animals/hyenas" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ifaw.org">[ifaw.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ifaw.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">HyenasPortrayed as aggressive predators, persistent scavengers, and threats to human life, hyenas are highly misunderstood animals. For anyone interested in Jordan’s mystery-animal traditions, the striped hyena offers an important lesson. Not every strange beast story begins with a hoax or an unknown creature. Sometimes it begins with a real animal that already looks unusual, moves mostly at night, appears near places people fear, and carries centuries of folklore on its back.

In that sense, the striped hyena may be one of Jordan’s most important “monster” animals—not because it is unknown, but because it shows exactly how ordinary wildlife can become extraordinary in the human imagination.
