## Introduction

Did a black panther roam [Bulgaria]({{ 'what-monsters-haunt-bulgarias-wild/' | relative_url }})? The short answer is that nobody knows for certain. In 2025, reports of a large black cat near the city of Shumen triggered one of the most unusual wildlife scares in modern Bulgarian history. Video footage, alleged tracks, intensive searches, police warnings and endless online discussion combined to create a classic “phantom cat” episode: a situation in which witnesses, authorities and the public all react to something that seems plausible but remains frustratingly difficult to prove.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2025/06/25/fact-checking-claims-about-the-panther-on-the-loose-in-bulgaria" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: euronews.com">[euronews]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">euronews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">fact checking claims about the panther on the loose in bulgaria</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fact-checking claims about the &#x27;panther&#x27; on the loose in...25 Jun 2025 — Bulgaria has been on high alert in recent days followin...</span></span></span>

## Shumen and Dupnitsa Report Clusters

The main wave of reports began in June 2025 around the Shumen Plateau in north-eastern Bulgaria. Authorities launched a large-scale search after a widely shared video appeared to show a dark, long-tailed feline moving through the area. Reports of tracks and possible prey remains added to the sense that something unusual might be present. Access restrictions were introduced in parts of the nature park while officials investigated.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2025/06/25/fact-checking-claims-about-the-panther-on-the-loose-in-bulgaria" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: euronews.com">[euronews.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">euronews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">fact checking claims about the panther on the loose in bulgaria</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fact-checking claims about the &#x27;panther&#x27; on the loose in...25 Jun 2025 — Bulgaria has been on high alert in recent days followin...</span></span></span>

The response was unusually serious. Search teams used drones, thermal-imaging equipment, camera traps and bait stations. Wildlife experts and hunting specialists became involved. Public warnings urged people to avoid certain areas until more was known.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.brusselstimes.com/1641998/bulgarians-on-the-lookout-for-mysterious-predator" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: brusselstimes.com">[The Brussels Times]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">brusselstimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">bulgarians on the lookout for mysterious predator</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Brussels TimesBulgarians on the lookout for mysterious predator23 Jun 2025 — Bulgaria is on high alert following the emergence of a v...</span></span></span>

Yet despite the intensity of the search, the evidence never became clearer. Days of fieldwork failed to produce a confirmed photograph, carcass, hair sample or reliable trackway. The mystery therefore moved into an uncomfortable middle ground: too much evidence to dismiss immediately, but too little to verify.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2025/07/11/bulgaria-wonders-where-the-elusive-black-panther-went-after-authorities-end-weeks-long-sea" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: euronews.com">[euronews]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">euronews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bulgaria wonders where the elusive black panther went...11 Jul 2025 — Authorities in Bulgaria have ended the active search for a...</span></span></span>

A second cluster of reports later emerged around the Dupnitsa region in western Bulgaria. Witnesses described seeing a large dark cat-like animal near fields and villages. Local officials treated the claims seriously enough to involve police patrols, but once again the reports remained unconfirmed and dependent largely on eyewitness testimony and disputed video evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.novinite.com/articles/234223/Back%2BFrom%2Bthe%2BShadows%3A%2BThe%2BBlack%2BPanther%2BRoams%2BBulgaria%2BOnce%2BMore%21%2B%28VIDEO%29" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: novinite.com">[Novinite]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">novinite.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Black Panther Roams Bulgaria Once More!</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">VIDEO)4 Sept 2025 — Reports of a large black predator, believed to be a leopard or a panther, stirred fear and heightened police activit...</span></span></span>

The result was a pattern familiar from phantom-cat cases elsewhere in Europe: an initial sighting generates publicity, publicity increases public awareness, and heightened awareness produces additional reports that may or may not involve the same animal.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lbc.co.uk/article/devon-cornwall-big-cat-sightings-police-2025-5Hjd6dp_2/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lbc.co.uk">[LBC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lbc.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">devon cornwall big cat sightings police 2025 5Hjd6dp 2</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">police reports reveal wave of UK big cat sightings &#124; LBC2 Jul 2025 — Reports recorded between January 2021 and March 2025 describe pan...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 2021</span></span></span>

## Why Phantom Cats Spread So Fast

One reason phantom-cat stories spread rapidly is that they sit in a believable zone between fantasy and reality.

A dragon in a forest can be dismissed immediately. A black leopard, however, is a real animal. People know such cats exist. They also know that exotic animals occasionally escape from private collections, zoos or illegal owners. That makes the claim feel possible even when evidence is weak.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.brusselstimes.com/1641998/bulgarians-on-the-lookout-for-mysterious-predator" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: brusselstimes.com">[The Brussels Times]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">brusselstimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">bulgarians on the lookout for mysterious predator</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Brussels TimesBulgarians on the lookout for mysterious predator23 Jun 2025 — Bulgaria is on high alert following the emergence of a v...</span></span></span>

The Shumen case gained additional momentum because it arrived with visual material. Even a brief or blurry video carries more persuasive power than a simple verbal report. Once the footage circulated online, discussion expanded far beyond the original witnesses. News outlets, social media users and commentators began examining every new image, rumour and claimed sighting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2025/06/25/fact-checking-claims-about-the-panther-on-the-loose-in-bulgaria" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: euronews.com">[euronews]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">euronews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">fact checking claims about the panther on the loose in bulgaria</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fact-checking claims about the &#x27;panther&#x27; on the loose in...25 Jun 2025 — Bulgaria has been on high alert in recent days followin...</span></span></span>

The episode also demonstrated how modern folklore develops. Within days, memes, jokes and altered images began circulating alongside genuine reporting. Fact-checkers found that some photographs shared online were unrelated stock images or mislabelled pictures from elsewhere. The mystery became partly about a possible animal and partly about the public's attempt to interpret a flood of information.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2025/06/25/fact-checking-claims-about-the-panther-on-the-loose-in-bulgaria" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: euronews.com">[euronews]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">euronews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">fact checking claims about the panther on the loose in bulgaria</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fact-checking claims about the &#x27;panther&#x27; on the loose in...25 Jun 2025 — Bulgaria has been on high alert in recent days followin...</span></span></span>

This mixture of genuine uncertainty and rapid online amplification is one reason phantom-cat reports remain common across Europe. Similar stories have appeared in Britain, Serbia, Italy and other countries, often producing far more sightings than confirmed evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lbc.co.uk/article/devon-cornwall-big-cat-sightings-police-2025-5Hjd6dp_2/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lbc.co.uk">[LBC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lbc.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">devon cornwall big cat sightings police 2025 5Hjd6dp 2</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">police reports reveal wave of UK big cat sightings &#124; LBC2 Jul 2025 — Reports recorded between January 2021 and March 2025 describe pan...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 2021</span></span></span>

## Possible Animals, Escapes and Mistakes

Several explanations were proposed for the Bulgarian reports.

The most dramatic possibility was that a melanistic leopard or jaguar—animals commonly called black panthers—had escaped from captivity. Police and commentators repeatedly discussed the possibility that an illegally kept exotic cat had been released or had escaped from a private owner. Such explanations have historical precedents because escaped exotic felines have occasionally been documented elsewhere in Europe.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.brusselstimes.com/1641998/bulgarians-on-the-lookout-for-mysterious-predator" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: brusselstimes.com">[brusselstimes.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">brusselstimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">bulgarians on the lookout for mysterious predator</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Brussels TimesBulgarians on the lookout for mysterious predator23 Jun 2025 — Bulgaria is on high alert following the emergence of a v...</span></span></span>

Another possibility was simple misidentification. Large dogs can appear surprisingly cat-like at a distance, especially in poor lighting or when viewed briefly. As the investigation continued, zoologists from Bulgaria's National Museum of Natural History publicly questioned whether the reported tracks were feline at all. Some specialists concluded that at least the most publicised paw prints were more consistent with a dog than a big cat.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2025/07/11/bulgaria-wonders-where-the-elusive-black-panther-went-after-authorities-end-weeks-long-sea" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: euronews.com">[euronews.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">euronews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bulgaria wonders where the elusive black panther went...11 Jul 2025 — Authorities in Bulgaria have ended the active search for a...</span></span></span>

Witness psychology may also have played a role. Once people become aware of a reported predator, ordinary observations can be reinterpreted through that lens. A distant dog, a shadow crossing a field or a fleeting glimpse of wildlife can seem much more significant when the possibility of a panther is already in the public imagination.
