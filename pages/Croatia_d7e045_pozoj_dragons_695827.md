## Introduction

In northern [Croatia]({{ 'what-monsters-does-croatia-remember/' | relative_url }}), some of the country's most famous dragon stories are not about creatures seen flying across the sky or lurking in remote lakes. They are about enormous dragons sleeping beneath towns. These beings are known as pozoj dragons, and according to local tradition they lie deep underground beneath places such as Zagreb and Čakovec. When they stir, the earth shakes. When they move, floods, storms or other disturbances may follow. The legends are not evidence for unknown animals, but they offer a fascinating glimpse into how earlier communities explained earthquakes, hidden water and other forces they could not fully understand. ## Why Do Croatian Dragons Sleep Underground?

Unlike many European dragon tales, the pozoj is usually imagined beneath the ground rather than guarding treasure in a cave or terrorising villages from the air. In Croatian folklore, especially in the north-western region of Međimurje, the pozoj is a gigantic serpent-like dragon associated with the underworld, hidden waters and unstable ground. Folklore researchers have noted connections between the pozoj and older Slavic mythological ideas in which serpent or dragon beings occupy the world beneath human settlements.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/61091914/Dragon_and_Hero_or_How_to_Kill_a_Dragon_on_the_Example_of_the_Legends_of_Me%C4%91imurje_about_the_Grabancija%C5%A1_and_the_DragonZmaj_i_junak_ili_kako_ubiti_zmaja_na_primjeru_me%C4%91imurskih_predaja_o_grabancija%C5%A1u_i_pozoju" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Academia(PDF) Dragon and Hero or How to Kill a DragonThe paper explores the depiction of dragons within the folklore of Međimurje, partic...</span></span></span>

The underground setting is important. Northern Croatia contains river plains, wetlands and areas historically prone to flooding. Long before modern geology and seismology, it was natural for people to imagine that something enormous lived beneath the landscape. A sleeping dragon offered a memorable explanation for sudden tremors, strange sounds, sinkholes or unpredictable water behaviour. Modern folklorists describe these stories as attempts to give shape and meaning to natural forces rather than records of actual creatures.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nomadit.co.uk/conference/isfnr2026/paper/91802" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nomadit.co.uk">[NomadIT]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nomadit.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Meteorological Myth and Earthquake Myth, or How It Was...13 Jun 2026 — Before seismology, people believed earthquakes were caused...</span></span></span>

## Zagreb's Sleeping Dragon and Earthquake Memory

The best-known urban version of the legend places a pozoj beneath Zagreb. According to popular stories, the dragon's head lies beneath the historic centre near the cathedral while its body stretches far beneath the city. Whenever the dragon shifts position or shrugs in its sleep, the ground trembles and earthquakes occur.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lovezagreb.hr/untold-stories/mysterious-world-of-zagreb-legends" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lovezagreb.hr">[Lovezagreb]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lovezagreb.hr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Mysterious World of Zagreb LegendsThe dragon sleeps under the city with his head under the old town, near the cathedral. The story became especially associated with earthquake memory. Zagreb suffered a devastating earthquake in 1880 that badly damaged the cathedral and transformed the city's appearance through subsequent reconstruction. Later generations connected the idea of the sleeping dragon with these destructive events, turning geological history into folklore.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagreb_Cathedral" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Zagreb Cathedral</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Zagreb Cathedral</span></span></span>

The legend gained renewed visibility after the earthquakes that struck Zagreb and central Croatia in 2020. Folklore scholars observed that the old dragon explanation resurfaced in public discussion, not because people literally believed a dragon caused the shaking, but because the image remained a powerful cultural metaphor. In moments of uncertainty, traditional stories offered a familiar way to talk about natural disasters.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nomadit.co.uk/conference/isfnr2026/paper/91802" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nomadit.co.uk">[NomadIT]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nomadit.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Meteorological Myth and Earthquake Myth, or How It Was...13 Jun 2026 — Before seismology, people believed earthquakes were caused...</span></span></span>

## The Pozoj Beneath Čakovec and Međimurje

If Zagreb has the most famous urban dragon, Čakovec has the most detailed pozoj tradition. Local legends place a gigantic green dragon beneath the town, often with its head under the church and its tail beneath the castle or town square, though the exact locations vary between tellings. The creature was blamed for earthquakes, hailstorms and other natural disturbances.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.turistickeprice.hr/en/pozoj-cakovec-legenda-medjimurje/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: turistickeprice.hr">[turistickeprice.hr]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">turistickeprice.hr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">pozoj cakovec legenda medjimurje</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Turisticke PriceThe legend of the dragon that guards the spirit of Međimurje29 Nov 2025 — In the very heart of Međimurje, just beneath th...</span></span></span>

A recurring figure in these stories is the grabancijaš, a wandering scholar or magical student. According to folklore, only this unusual hero possessed the knowledge needed to control the dragon. In many versions he places a golden bridle on the pozoj and rides it away from the town, restoring peace. Folklore researchers view this as part of a wider Central European tradition in which learned magical figures confront storm-bringing dragons.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%8Cakovec" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[wikipedia.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on wikipedia.org.</span></span></span>

The Čakovec dragon became so closely tied to local identity that it remains a recognised symbol of the region. ## How Landscape Legends Explain Shaking Ground and Water

One of the most interesting features of pozoj lore is how consistently it links dragons with environmental processes.

Several recurring themes appear across northern Croatian traditions:

* **Earthquakes** were explained as the movement of a sleeping dragon beneath the earth.
* **Floods and marshes** were associated with hidden underground creatures controlling water.
* **Storms and hail** were sometimes attributed to dragons rising into the sky or battling supernatural opponents.
* **Churches and castles** frequently appear in the stories because they were the most prominent landmarks in towns and provided fixed points for locating an otherwise invisible creature.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nomadit.co.uk/conference/isfnr2026/paper/91802" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nomadit.co.uk">[nomadit.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nomadit.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Meteorological Myth and Earthquake Myth, or How It Was...13 Jun 2026 — Before seismology, people believed earthquakes were caused...</span></span></span>

The result is a folklore mechanism that makes natural hazards easier to imagine. Instead of abstract geological forces, communities pictured a living being beneath their feet. The dragon gave earthquakes a face, a motive and a story.

## Folklore, Not Cryptozoology

From a cryptid perspective, the pozoj occupies an unusual position. There are very few modern claims of people actually seeing such creatures. The tradition is centred on explanation rather than observation. Unlike lake monsters or mystery cats, the pozoj is remembered because of where it supposedly sleeps and what it supposedly causes, not because witnesses regularly report encounters.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.turistickeprice.hr/en/pozoj-cakovec-legenda-medjimurje/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: turistickeprice.hr">[Turisticke Price]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">turistickeprice.hr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">pozoj cakovec legenda medjimurje</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Turisticke PriceThe legend of the dragon that guards the spirit of Međimurje29 Nov 2025 — In the very heart of Međimurje, just beneath th...</span></span></span>

For that reason, historians and folklorists generally treat the pozoj as a legendary being rather than a candidate unknown animal. Academic studies connect the dragon to broader Slavic mythological traditions involving underworld serpents, seasonal cycles and conflicts between supernatural forces.
